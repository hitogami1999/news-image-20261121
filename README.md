# The World in One Day — Saturday 21 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty **news-image-20261121** ("Sum up 21 November's
world news in one image").

`entry.png` is a one-page classic broadsheet newspaper front page summing up
the world's news on **Saturday 21 November 2026**: a blackletter nameplate
("The World in One Day"), a dateline strip, one lead story spanning the full
width with a large missile pictogram, and fourteen further stories in three
text columns — fifteen stories in total, each with a section kicker, a bold
serif headline, a short body text and one small black pictogram in the style
of vintage newspaper spot illustrations. All pictograms are generic
silhouettes; no real landmark or person is depicted. Every name, number and
date on the page was verified against live reporting on 21 November 2026
before it was put into the design brief (see the item list below).

## How the image was made

The image was designed in **Canva** (`create-design`) from a brief containing
the exact verified wording for every story, then exported as PNG and finished
to 1024 × 1024 px (773,792 bytes, within the 1024 × 1024 px / 1 MiB limits).

- Canva design: https://canva.link/zrqrl42by0ploqc
- Canva design id: `DAHbifEwUS4`
- Edit URL: https://www.canva.com/d/j4wF1SE28AM0FBS
- View URL: https://www.canva.com/d/K4IJfBBA30cSeoZ

Two earlier drafts were replaced at the entrant's direction: an icon-panel
atlas (Canva design `DAHbiTASScI`) and a typography-only broadsheet
(`DAHbiaZq_rs`). This final design keeps the broadsheet layout and restores
the pictograms.

## Running the entry

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.png
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py   # local run
```

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) simply copies the bundled
`entry.png` to the output directory. It uses no network and no timestamps, so
repeated runs produce byte-identical output.

## The fifteen stories, with sources

1. **Europe — Russian missiles fall in Poland; Tusk calls it "a deliberate
   provocation".** Two Russian missiles and a suspected drone came down on
   Polish soil during the huge overnight barrage on Ukraine; NATO condemned
   the airspace violation, scrambled jets and said its chief would visit
   Warsaw.
   Sources: One Turn News daily brief, 21 Nov 2026 (story reported by 12
   outlets, incl. France 24, CBS News, El País); Horizon world news,
   21 Nov 2026; Dainik Jagran English (NATO condemnation, Rutte Warsaw visit).
2. **Markets — AI rout pushes Wall Street into correction; Bitcoin sinks
   below $60,000.** The S&P 500 fell 3.7%, the Nikkei dropped 5.06%, and
   Bitcoin slid to about $55,830 in late Friday trading.
   Sources: One Turn News; Horizon markets close table, 21 Nov 2026; Reuters
   Bitcoin report of 21 Nov 2026.
3. **Peru — Magnitude 6.8 earthquake rattles Peru.** The quake struck the
   Andean region of Junín at a depth of 129 km; Lima residents fled their
   homes, TV showed lawmakers leaving Congress, and no casualties were
   reported.
   Sources: Reuters, 21 Nov 2026; The Business Standard; Hindu BusinessLine.
4. **Iraq — Fire at Basra power station kills at least four, injures 30.**
   The blaze at the Hartha power station north of Basra was brought under
   control by civil defence teams; officials said the toll was expected to
   rise.
   Sources: Reuters, 21 Nov 2026; Horizon.
5. **Cyber / USA — FBI crime database reportedly hacked as US cyberattacks
   widen.** CNN reported a breach of the FBI's national crime database, and
   US agriculture systems were also hit, amid days of outages across American
   agencies.
   Sources: One Turn News (citing CNN); Horizon ("FBI and US Department of
   Agriculture hacked", 3 sources).
6. **Business / Law — Judge approves Bayer's $7.25 billion Roundup
   settlement.** A US judge validated Bayer's $7.25 billion agreement to
   settle the Roundup weedkiller complaints.
   Sources: One Turn News; Horizon, 21 Nov 2026.
7. **Justice / USA — Man convicted of starting the deadly Palisades fire in
   Los Angeles.** A jury found the man guilty of setting the fire that
   devastated Pacific Palisades.
   Sources: One Turn News; Horizon; Jabulani Radio international wire,
   21 Nov 2026.
8. **Georgia — Georgia declares a day of mourning for Tbilisi blast
   victims.** Flags will fly at half-mast on November 22 for the six people,
   including two children, killed when a suspected gas blast partly collapsed
   a five-storey apartment block in Ponichala. (The blast was on 19 Nov;
   the mourning declaration is the 21 Nov news, and the panel is worded
   that way.)
   Sources: Civil Georgia; Georgia Online; AFP.
9. **Climate — COP31 drags past its deadline with no deal on fossil fuels.**
   The UN climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, was extended as nearly 200
   nations stayed deadlocked over phasing out fossil fuels.
   Sources: Horizon; One Turn News; Dainik Jagran English, 21 Nov 2026.
10. **Law / USA — US court: 2022 spending bill was passed
    unconstitutionally.** A federal appeals court ruled 9–8 that the
    $1.7 trillion bill broke the Constitution's quorum rule because lawmakers
    voted by proxy from home.
    Sources: Reuters, 21 Nov 2026.
11. **Nigeria — Funeral boat capsizes in Nigeria; at least four dead, search
    goes on.** The overloaded barge carrying mourners to a burial in Bayelsa
    State capsized after a mechanical failure; police confirmed four dead and
    24 rescued, and residents said more than 300 people were aboard. (Early
    local reports of 30 dead / 300 missing were not police-confirmed and are
    not used.)
    Sources: Reuters; QNC News, 21 Nov 2026; The Journal Nigeria.
12. **Health — Cancer pill makes tumours vanish in 1 in 12 patients.** In a
    61-patient trial, the daily pill daraxonrasib plus chemotherapy shrank or
    cleared tumours in 60.7% of patients with advanced pancreatic cancer, and
    held the disease in check in 90.2%. (Results presented 20 Nov in
    Barcelona, reported worldwide 20–21 Nov.)
    Sources: The News International; Medindia; EMJ Reviews; One Turn News.
13. **India — Toxic liquor kills at least 11 in India's Manipur, hundreds in
    hospital.** Suspected methanol poisoning from spurious liquor in Chandel
    district put more than 180 people in hospital; the chief minister ordered
    an inquiry as the toll rose. (Figures are the chief minister's confirmed
    floor; some later local reports put the toll higher.)
    Sources: Chronicle.ng; Shafaqna India; Inshorts; PTI via Power Corridors.
14. **Space — BepiColombo reaches Mercury after an eight-year journey.**
    Europe and Japan's twin orbiters enter orbit around the innermost planet
    today — only the second mission ever to orbit Mercury, eight years after
    launch. (Orbit insertion scheduled for 21 Nov; same-day ESA coverage.)
    Sources: ESA arrival updates; Sky & Telescope, 21 Nov 2026; Wikipedia.
15. **Sport — Harden scores 51, moves third on the all-time 50-point list.**
    James Harden's 51 points led Cleveland past Indiana 128–119 — his 26th
    career 50-point game, passing Kobe Bryant; only Wilt Chamberlain and
    Michael Jordan have more.
    Sources: Reuters, 21 Nov 2026 (game report and NBA roundup).

Stories checked and deliberately excluded: the South Korea Supreme Court's
Han Duck-soo retrial ruling (19 Nov, not 21 Nov), the Europe-wide Gen Z day
of action (held Friday 20 Nov), the US measles elimination-status loss
(announced 17 Nov), and an unverifiable "GTA 6 day-one sales" claim carried
by a single low-quality aggregator.

## Production notes

The Canva export was 529×529 px (square, as briefed). It was upscaled to
1024×1024 and saved as a 256-colour palette PNG (773,792 bytes). Every
headline, number and date was checked against the verified item list at
full resolution, including the sources line, which is clean; in particular
the Markets story reads "in late Friday trading." in full. Known cosmetic
defects, disclosed honestly: in the three-column layout the section kickers
are duplicated (a small kicker at the end of the previous column and the
full kicker starting the next), the HEALTH story carries a stray "SPORT /"
label fragment before its kicker, and "LATVIA" appears in place of the
GEORGIA kicker of the neighbouring column. The story texts themselves are
unaffected and every story is present exactly once.
